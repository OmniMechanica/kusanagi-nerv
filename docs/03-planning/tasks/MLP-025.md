# MLP-025 — GPU MIG, fragmentation and scale economics

**Project:** KUSANAGI NERV  
**Priority:** P2  
**Schedule:** Stretch (unscheduled)  
**Learning/build timebox:** 4–8 h (estimate, not a deadline)  
**Status:** Planned  
**Dependencies:** [MLP-016](MLP-016.md), [MLP-022](MLP-022.md)

## Objective

Quantify and reason about GPU sharing/packing economics.

## Implementation steps

- Identify compatible GPUs and MIG profile limitations.
- Contrast MIG, time slicing and dedicated allocations for isolation/throughput.
- Measure or simulate scheduling fragmentation for job sizes and capacity mixes.
- Write cost/scale tradeoffs and limits of the experiment.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Scheduling examples and tradeoffs documented.
- [ ] Hardware-specific claims are measured only on compatible GPUs; otherwise DESIGN-ONLY.

## Required evidence

DESIGN-ONLY default; TESTED-GPU only with hardware logs.

## What to study in parallel

NVIDIA MIG documentation and Kubernetes scheduler/resource constraints.

## Interview drill

How would you place 1-, 2-, and 4-GPU jobs with minimal fragmentation and fairness?

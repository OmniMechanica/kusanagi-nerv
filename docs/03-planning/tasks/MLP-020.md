# MLP-020 — Controlled chaos drill and 60-minute platform design mock

**Project:** KUSANAGI NERV  
**Priority:** P0  
**Schedule:** Day 20  
**Learning/build timebox:** 3–5 h (estimate, not a deadline)  
**Status:** Planned  
**Dependencies:** [MLP-007](MLP-007.md), [MLP-010](MLP-010.md), [MLP-013](MLP-013.md)

## Objective

Validate critical failure assumptions and defend architecture under interview pressure.

The CPU drill does not require the P1 hardening tasks or completed GPU design. [MLP-016](MLP-016.md) is recommended preparation for the GPU portion of the discussion and remains required for the final rehearsal.

## Implementation steps

- Inject controller crash near submission and inspect lease/reconciliation.
- Inject training worker failure after checkpoint; verify resume.
- Inject temporary storage failure or missing GPU capacity (simulation/design permitted, labelled).
- Run one 60-minute platform system design using scale/SLO/fairness prompts.
- Capture observed results, blind spots and concrete hardening actions.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] At least two actual failure cases have reproducible evidence.
- [ ] Correct behavior under cancel/retry/reconciliation is explained.
- [ ] System design has a score and identified improvement areas.
- [ ] Any unexecuted GPU failure is explicitly DESIGN-ONLY.

## Required evidence

TESTED-CPU for worker/controller chaos, optional GPU, plus scorecard.

## What to study in parallel

`docs/02-architecture/reliability.md` and interview question bank.

## Interview drill

How do you prevent uncontrolled duplicate launches? How do you discover jobs stuck between DB and Ray?

## Definition of Done

Pass applicable checks in [Definition of Done](../../06-engineering/definition-of-done.md); log result and evidence in [progress](../progress.md). Do not call design-only work a successful hardware run.
